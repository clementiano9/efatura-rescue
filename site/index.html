Filled in like a form, one quadro at a time

For each invoice, the extension fills four boxes. Only the third needs a judgement, and that one is Jev’s.

  1. Quadro 1Read
    Emitente · IVAMetropolitano Lisboa EP6% · 2,26 € of 40,00 €

    It opens the invoice with your own session and reads the VAT lines. e-Fatura keeps no item names, only amounts and rates.

  2. Quadro 2Look up
    Atividade (CAE)49310Urban passenger transport

    nif.pt says what the merchant does. New merchants queue, because the free key allows 10 lookups an hour.

  3. Quadro 3Decide
    CategoriaPasse público · Jev 100%

    Jev picks one of the 16 categories. Above 0.8 confidence it’s stamped. Below, it gets a pencil mark and is asked again with the merchant’s other invoices.

  4. Quadro 4Count
    Dedução+ 2,26 €

    Each stamped invoice adds to a running euro total, using the 2025 IRS rates and caps.

“A meal or snack eaten on site at a café or restaurant: food at 13% VAT, often with a drink at 23%.”

Jev writes no text of its own. Each category is offered to it as a full sentence like this one, and the sentence it picks is the reason printed under the invoice.

Submeter is the one stamp it doesn’t own.

It stamps what it’s sure of. Saving your categories stays a click you make.

Submeter

Kept off the rack on purpose.

  • Never answers “Todas”.When one merchant has several invoices, the portal asks whether to give them all the same sector. The extension answers “Apenas esta”, because each invoice is decided on its own.
  • Clicks only a button whose label matches.If the portal relabels a button, the extension leaves it alone.
  • Your pick wins.Invoices you’ve already set are skipped.
  • Sends only what it needs.The invoice’s fields go to Jev and the merchant’s NIF goes to nif.pt. Nothing else leaves your browser.

Before your first pass

  1. 50 invoices at a time. The portal lists the 50 most recent pending invoices, so a bigger backlog takes a few passes.
  2. New merchants queue. The free nif.pt key allows 10 lookups an hour. Their invoices keep the pencil mark until the lookup lands.
  3. Books, arts and museums count €0. Those categories are stamped, but their deduction rates aren’t confirmed, so the total leaves them out.
  4. Later pages aren’t proven. Whether Submeter saves picks on list pages you’re not viewing hasn’t been checked on the real portal.

Get the developer preview

It isn’t on the Chrome Web Store yet. Loading it by hand takes three steps, a Jev key and a free nif.pt key.

View the code on GitHub
  1. Add your keysA Jev key (through OpenRouter or TypeSafe) and a free nif.pt key, typed into the extension’s Settings page.
  2. Load it in ChromeOpen chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder from the GitHub repo.
  3. Open your pending invoicesGo to “resolver pendências” on e-Fatura with page translation off, then press Sort pending invoices.
